title: Changelog - Dec 04, 2012
---

* You can now right-click to delete files/folders from the IDE sidepane
* The IDE denotes "dirty" status of files that you edit &ndash; you are also warned if you try to close a "dirty" file
* 'New' and 'Close' menus and key-bindings are now context-aware, i.e.  if you are in the console, 'New' opens a new console session.
* Keyboard binding to refresh the sidepane
* All boxes come bundled with C libraries for mysql and postgres, so you can connect to your databases using your favorite Ruby/Python/Node.JS/Go client libraries.
* All boxes also come installed with other utilities such as traceroute, ack-grep, silver-searcher and more.
* You can now restart your boxes

We've made more scalability and performance improvements, so expect more
invites to be sent in the next few weeks!

There's more coming in the month of December, so stay tuned!

of changes.
