* Support for Go (with Google App Engine SDK)
* Heroku comes bundled with every box - [deployment is now super-easy](http://help.action.io/customer/portal/articles/912877-heroku-integration)

* [GitHub OAuth Support](http://help.action.io/customer/portal/articles/853510-adding-ssh-keys-to-github) &ndash; You can now upload your public key to
  GitHub with one click and clone your repos to action.io

We have more exciting changes in the pipeline, so stay tuned!
