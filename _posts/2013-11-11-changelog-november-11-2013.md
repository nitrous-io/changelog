---

[Collaboration](http://help.nitrous.io/collab/) got some love this week. Try it with a colleague out if you haven't!

* You can now see your collaborators' cursor positions in the IDE when doing collaborative editing.
* @reply chat mentions will now play a notification sound.
* If you get a @reply chat mention but aren't online, you get an email notification about it.

* Added the option to download files from IDE.

If you have any questions or suggestions, you can [talk to a human](mailto: support@nitrous.io).

Don't forget to follow us on [Twitter](https://www.twitter.com/nitrousio) and subscribe to our [Updates Blog](http://feeds.feedburner.com/actionio-updates) to hear about new product releases.
