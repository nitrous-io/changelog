title: Changelog - November 22, 2013
---

More updates have arrived. Lots of improvements with collaboration, but the best is yet to come. Stay tuned to [@nitrousio](http://www.twitter.com/nitrousio) for updates and other great news.

* You can now email collaborators within the chat window. If you type `@username` and the user is not currently connected to the box, that user will receive an email.
* When collaborating on a file, each user's cursor with be a unique color. Their username will also display above when editing code.
* Text editor has been updated for CoffeeScript indentation fix.
* Added more language syntax highlighting support within the editor (ex. Groovy).
* Introduced Gravatar integration for collaboration. You can add your own avatar at [http://en.gravatar.com/](http://en.gravatar.com/).
* You now have the ability to add basic_auth password protection to preview link.

If you have any questions or suggestions, you can [talk to a human](mailto: support@nitrous.io).

Don't forget to follow us on [Twitter](http://www.twitter.com/nitrousio) and subscribe to our [Updates Blog](http://feeds.feedburner.com/actionio-updates) to hear about new product releases.
