We'll be updating our changelog every 1-2 weeks, sorry for the radio silence! Some news if you've been living under a rock:

* [Changed our name](http://blog.nitrous.io/2013/04/16/actionio-is-now-nitrousio.html) from Action.IO to Nitrous.IO
* Collaborative code editing alpha
* Automatic reconnection in Web IDE
* International keyboard support
* Improved performance on IDE by > 30%, much faster connections
* New [support site](http://help.nitrous.io), we intend to let you submit pull requests soon
* Improved iPad support (work in progress)

And much more that we'll be announcing in a few weeks...stay tuned.

Don't forget to follow us on [Twitter](http://twitter.com/nitrousio) and
subscribe to our [Updates blog](http://feeds.feedburner.com/actionio-updates) to keep track
of changes.
