More update goodness, lots of improvements to the Web IDE and continued stability and performance improvements. Some bigger items coming soon, stay tuned to [@nitrousio](http://www.twitter.com/nitrousio) for updates!

* Sign up / Sign in with your Github & Google accounts
* File status support in the collaborative coding chat window. E.g.: "Pete opened [hello.js](/#)"
* Improved stability of web previewing functionality
* Additional keyboard shortcuts and improvements in the Web IDE including:
  * cmd+c and cmd+v support for copy and paste in mac
  * cmd+c and cmd+v support for copy and paste in mac
  * ctrl+shift+v support to paste on chrome,  paste using menu bar in firefox
* Fixed miscellaneous text selection issues in the console on the web IDE.

If you have any questions or suggestions, you can [talk to a human](mailto: support@nitrous.io).

Don't forget to follow us on [Twitter](http://www.twitter.com/nitrousio) and subscribe to our [Updates Blog](http://feeds.feedburner.com/actionio-updates) to hear about new product releases.
