Things have been very exciting this week! Check out the goods delivered by [@nitrousio](http://www.twitter.com/nitrousio).

* Launched a Chrome Packaged App in the [Chrome Store](https://chrome.google.com/webstore/detail/nitrousio/efdcneeepllhjlbejkfnaolelbpdacai/)
* Added support for inotify. You can now monitor system events and use features such as fswatch and Sass
* Fixed a port issue which was preventing connections to certain external databases
* You can now signup with 2 and 3 character usernames
* Updated to postgres 9.2

If you have any questions or suggestions, you can [talk to a human](mailto: support@nitrous.io).

Don't forget to follow us on [Twitter](http://www.twitter.com/nitrousio) and subscribe to our [Updates Blog](http://feeds.feedburner.com/actionio-updates) to hear about new product releases.
