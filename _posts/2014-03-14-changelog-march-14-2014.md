---

It's getting so much better all the time...

* [PHP Templates](http://help.nitrous.io/php/) are now available on the boxes page. This includes PHP5, Apache2, Composer, and other PHP related tools. Get started with frameworks such as Laravel and Phalcon by utilizing Composer.
* Nitrous Desktop for Windows arrived this month. [Download it now](https://www.nitrous.io/desktop#windows) and start using your favorite locat text editor on Windows. Check out the Nitrous Desktop help guide for other features such as port forwarding.
* Custom Domain support has been added to Shift and Turbo packages. Read our [support guide](http://help.nitrous.io/custom-domains/) for more information on this.
* More parts have been added to [Autoparts](http://help.nitrous.io/autoparts). This includes Node.JS, Go, Google AppEngine, Cloud Foundry, Zsh, Tcsh, Fish, PHPUnit, PHPMyAdmin, Beanstalk, and more.

If you have any questions or suggestions, you can [talk to a human](mailto: support@nitrous.io).

Don't forget to follow us on [Twitter](http://www.twitter.com/nitrousio) and subscribe to our [Updates Blog](http://feeds.feedburner.com/actionio-updates) to hear about new product releases.
