Another week, another batch of sweet improvements to [@nitrousio](http://www.twitter.com/nitrousio).  We have some big projects in the works, so stay tuned.

* iPad improvements. See what features are now available on our [blog post](http://blog.nitrous.io/2013/08/08/ipad-support-cometh.html)
* Launched a Chrome Packaged App in the [Chrome Store](https://chrome.google.com/webstore/detail/nitrousio/efdcneeepllhjlbejkfnaolelbpdacai/)
* Updated to git 1.7.10 and ruby 2.0.0-p247 on Ruby box
* Added notifications when account is running low on N2O or hitting memory / storage limits. 
* Added more ways to earn resources on the [N2O Bonus Page](https://www.nitrous.io/app#/n2o/bonus)

If you have any questions or suggestions, you can [talk to a human](mailto: support@nitrous.io).

Don't forget to follow us on [Twitter](http://www.twitter.com/nitrousio) and subscribe to our [Updates Blog](http://feeds.feedburner.com/actionio-updates) to hear about new product releases.
