---

We have been quiet the last month, but only because we were working hard on new updates. Take a look at the new features we now offer.

* Payments are now available! Take a look at the [pricing page](https://www.nitrous.io/pricing) to see what features are offered.
* SSL Support is now available for all [paid accounts](https://www.nitrous.io/pricing).
* You can now manage [Autoparts](http://help.nitrous.io/autoparts/) within the UI. Read the [blog post](http://blog.nitrous.io/2014/01/17/intro-autopartsui-previewssl.html) for more information.
* Extra CPU share is given to certain [paid accounts](https://www.nitrous.io/pricing).
* The [help site](http://help.nitrous.io/) has a new layout for easier navigation.
* You can now keep your boxes running at all times with any [paid account](https://www.nitrous.io/pricing).
* Have a business or a team? Fill out our [business survey](https://www.nitrous.io/business) for extra N2O.

If you have any questions or suggestions, you can [talk to a human](mailto: support@nitrous.io).

Don't forget to follow us on [Twitter](http://www.twitter.com/nitrousio) and subscribe to our [Updates Blog](http://feeds.feedburner.com/actionio-updates) to hear about new product releases.
