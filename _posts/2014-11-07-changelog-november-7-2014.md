Many more improvements have arrived! Follow [@nitrousio](http://www.twitter.com/nitrousio) for more news, or check out our [blog](http://blog.nitrous.io).

* Box Snapshots have been added to Shift and Turbo packages. Read the [blog post](http://blog.nitrous.io/2014/10/30/announcing-private-box-snapshots-backup-your-dev-environments.html) for more details.
* Added [Meteor 1.0 templates](http://blog.nitrous.io/2014/10/28/meteor-template-on-nitrous-io.html) for instant Meteor development. Have a Meteor project you want to share with the world? Use the [hack button](https://www.nitrous.io/embed) to allow developers to run your app in seconds.
* Increased the base configuration of Nitrous boxes to include 512MB of memeory, and 1.5GB of storage.
* Updated [Elixir](https://github.com/nitrous-io/autoparts/blob/master/lib/autoparts/packages/elixir.rb) to v1.0.2 within [Autoparts](https://github.com/nitrous-io/autoparts). Thanks [edubkendo](https://github.com/edubkendo) for the contribution!
Books
* [Purescript 0.5.7.1](https://github.com/nitrous-io/autoparts/blob/master/lib/autoparts/packages/purescript.rb) has been added to Autoparts. Thanks [cschneid](https://github.com/cschneid)!

If you have any questions or suggestions, you can [talk to a human](mailto: support@nitrous.io).

Don't forget to subscribe to our [Blog's RSS Feed](http://feeds.feedburner.com/actionio-updates) to hear about new product releases.
