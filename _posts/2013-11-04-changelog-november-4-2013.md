---

It has been a while since the last update as we have been working on large infrastructure upgrades. However, we do have some new features to keep you entertained.

* WebSocket support has been added. Take a look at the [support documentation](http://help.nitrous.io/websockets/) for examples and other information.
* When previewing an app running on port 3000, it is automatically forwarded to port 80. If you are running behind a firewall, try running the app on port 3000.
* Enabled ability to add basic auth to preview.
* Added Lua and Rust to [Autoparts](https://github.com/action-io/autoparts).

If you have any questions or suggestions, you can [talk to a human](mailto: support@nitrous.io).

Don't forget to follow us on [Twitter](http://www.twitter.com/nitrousio) and subscribe to our [Updates Blog](http://feeds.feedburner.com/actionio-updates) to hear about new product releases.
